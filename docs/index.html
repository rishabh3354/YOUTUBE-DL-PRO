Frequently asked questions

Is DL-YouTube free?

It is free to download and use. A one-time Pro upgrade removes limits and unlocks the full feature set — there is no subscription.

Does it work on Linux?

Yes, Linux is its primary platform. Install it from Flathub, the Snap Store or as a portable AppImage on Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, Arch, Linux Mint and Pop!_OS.

Which sites does it support?

More than 1400, including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), Twitch, Vimeo, Reddit, SoundCloud, Rumble and Odysee.

How do I download a video as MP3?

Paste the link, switch the output to audio, pick MP3 or FLAC, M4A or Opus, and download. Only the audio track is saved.

Can it download a whole playlist?

Yes. Paste a playlist URL and every video in it is queued for download.

Does it download subtitles?

Yes. Subtitle and closed-caption tracks can be saved alongside the video.

Does it contain ads, trackers or bundled software?

No. It ships from Flathub, the Snap Store and signed GitHub releases with no advertising, no analytics and no bundled installers.

How is it different from 4KTUBE?

DL-YouTube is the general-purpose downloader with the widest site coverage. 4KTUBE is the YouTube specialist, tuned for maximum resolution, playlists and in-app search.

Responsible use

DL-YouTube is a tool for saving content you have the right to save — your own uploads, Creative Commons material, public-domain video, and content you have permission to keep offline. Please respect copyright and the terms of service of the sites you download from.
